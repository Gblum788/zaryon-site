    Questions

    What ownersask us first.

    Will callers know they're talking to an AI?

    Yes. Every call opens by saying it's a virtual assistant and that the call is recorded. It never pretends to be a person.

    Do I have to change my phone number?

    No. You keep your number. You choose which calls the AI takes: after hours, overflow, or all of them.

    Will it quote prices or give legal advice?

    No. It gathers the details, books the appointment and passes everything to your team. Your people set prices and give advice.

    What languages does it speak?

    English, Spanish and Portuguese. It switches to the caller's language automatically, and owners can get summaries in their own language.

    Does it work with my CRM and calendar?

    Usually yes: JobNimbus, AccuLynx, ServiceTitan, HubSpot, GoHighLevel, Clio, Lawmatics, Google Calendar and more. We confirm during the audit.

    Do you work outside Florida?

    Yes. We're based in Clermont, Florida and work with businesses across the US, fully remote.
